Who we are

Ruin's Keep is made by Dark Dev, an independent game studio. In this policy, "we" means Dark Dev. You can reach us at [contact address to come].

What this policy covers

Ruin's Keep for Android (package name com.darkdev.ruinskeep), whether you got it from Google Play or as an .apk file from us. The ads described below are only in the Android version. The other versions we share, for Windows and as a web app, show no ads.

Your save stays on your device

The game keeps your progress in its own storage on your phone: your gear, coins, cleared stages, records, feats, runes, bestiary, looks, settings, and which tutorial hints you have seen. The save holds no name, no email address and no account. We have no server, so it is never sent to us.

Android's own backup. If you have turned on backup on your phone, Android may copy the game's data into your phone's backup in your Google Account, and put it back if you install the game again. That copy is made by Android, not by the game, and we cannot see it. Google explains how to turn backup on or off in Back up or restore data on your Android device.

Ads (Android only)

The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one, for one of these bonuses:

  • get back up when your health runs out, once a run (after your Second Wind skill, if you hold it);
  • reroll a skill pick, once a run;
  • double the coins from a run, once, when it ends.

No ad ever plays by itself, there are no banners, and the whole game can be played without watching any.

The ads come from Google AdMob. To find, show and count an ad, and to stop fraud, Google's ad software in the game collects:

  • Your device's advertising ID and its app set ID: numbers used for ads, which you can reset or delete (see Your advertising ID).
  • Your IP address, which Google can use to estimate roughly where you are: an area, not your exact place.
  • How you use the app and the ads: when the app opens, taps, and the ads you watch.
  • How the app and the ad software run on your device: how long the app takes to start, how often it freezes, and how much power it uses.

This data goes to Google, encrypted on the way. Google uses it under its own privacy policy: to show and measure ads, to stop fraud, and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. We do not receive it. AdMob gives us only totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned, and nothing that tells us who you are.

Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choice in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you play in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message before any ad is loaded. It asks whether Google may use your data for personalised ads and the other purposes it lists, and you can say no.

If you say no, Google may still show ads that are not personalised. Those still use some data, such as your IP address, to deliver the ad and to stop fraud.

You can change your answer at any time: on the home screen, open Settings, then Privacy choices.

Your advertising ID

Your phone lets you reset its advertising ID (it gets a new one) and, on Android 12 and newer, delete it (apps then get no advertising ID at all). Open your phone's Settings and find Ads: on most phones it is under Google or Privacy. Google's help: Advertising ID.

To see and change how Google personalises the ads you are shown, use My Ad Center.

The purchase

One optional purchase is planned: a one-time "remove ads", which gives the bonuses above without an ad. When it is on sale, Google Play takes the payment. We never see your card or bank details. Google tells us that an order was made (such as its number, its date and what was bought), with whatever else Google's own terms allow, so that we can handle refunds and taxes. We use it for nothing else. The payment itself is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Children

Ruin's Keep is not made for children under 13, and on Google Play it is listed for ages 13 and over. The ads in it are not set up for children. We collect nothing from anyone ourselves.

If you are a parent and your child under 13 has played it, uninstalling the game removes its save from the phone, and you can delete the phone's advertising ID as shown above. You can also write to us at [contact address to come].

Deleting your data

  • Your progress only: on the home screen, open Settings, then Erase progress. It deletes all gear, records and your class choice, and keeps your settings. It cannot be undone.
  • Everything on the phone: uninstall the game. That deletes the save and the settings from the phone (Android's own backup aside: see Your save).
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID, and use Google's own tools, such as My Ad Center and My Activity, for what Google holds.

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete. You can still ask us anything at [contact address to come].

What the app can reach on your phone

The game asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files. It goes online only for the ads and Google's consent message.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to your data protection authority. We hold none ourselves. For the data the ads send to Google, Google's privacy policy explains your rights and how to use them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new one goes up at this same address with a new date at the top. If a change means the game collects something new, the game's update notes on Google Play will say so.
